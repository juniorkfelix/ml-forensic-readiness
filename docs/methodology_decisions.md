every deviation from the original specification. Entries are append-only. When a decision is
revised, a new entry supersedes the old one; old entries are not edited.

| D-015 | 2026-09-27 | design | **Decision (researcher-approved)** | **Threat model: data-store tampering.** The pipeline registers the clean training dataset. | D-016 | 2026-09-27 | design | Decision | Pipeline C runs integrity checks in **record-only** mode. | D-017 | 2026-09-27 | design | Decision | The reconstruction engine may use **only** the evidence store of the pipeline being investigated plus the incident ticket. | D-018 | 2026-09-27 | design | Decision | Every investigation starts from an **incident ticket**: what an affected user or operator could report (timestamp, request ID, the input submitted, observed prediction, expected label). It contains no poisoned sample IDs or attack parameters. | Gives every pipeline the same fair starting point. | Equal entry point for all pipelines (§45). |
| D-019 | 2026-09-27 | design | Decision | Forensic loggers **preserve** evidence (manifests, hashes, check results); they do not **interpret** it. | D-022 | 2026-09-27 | 3 | Decision (supersedes D-013) | Batches are built **on the device** from uint8 images held in GPU memory. Crop, flip and normalisation run per batch on the GPU.
